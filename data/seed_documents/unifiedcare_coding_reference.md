## Scope and limitations

CMS NCCI edits are not automatically the rule for every commercial payer. Confirm the payer, program, provider type, place of service, quarterly edit file, and date of service before applying an edit or modifier.

The official NCCI program uses procedure-to-procedure (PTP) edits to identify code combinations that may represent incorrect coding and MUE edits to identify unlikely units. An edit is not, by itself, proof that services were clinically impossible or that a modifier is appropriate.

## Distinct procedural services

Modifier 59 may be appropriate only when documentation supports a distinct procedural service and the payer's rules permit its use. Use the more specific X modifiers when applicable:

- **XE:** separate encounter;
- **XS:** separate structure or anatomic site;
- **XP:** separate practitioner;
- **XU:** unusual non-overlapping service.

Do not append a modifier only to bypass an edit. The record should identify the distinct encounter, practitioner, site, structure, or non-overlapping service and explain why the procedures were separately reportable.

## Pre-submission checks

1. Confirm the current PTP/MUE file and payer policy for the date of service.
2. Compare the billed code pair, units, modifiers, diagnosis pointers, and place of service.
3. Confirm that the record supports separate reporting and that the service is not a duplicate.
4. Preserve the edit-file version, code descriptions, and source used for the decision.

## Denial patterns

- Duplicate or overlapping services reported for the same date of service.
- Unbundling denial where a current PTP edit applies.
- Modifier used without documentation of a distinct service.
- Units exceeding the applicable MUE or payer limit.

## Appeal evidence checklist

Provide the remittance denial, current edit-file reference, operative or procedure note excerpts, date/time and site information, separate encounter or practitioner evidence when relevant, diagnosis pointers, and a concise explanation of why the services were distinct. Do not cite NCCI as authority for a commercial payer unless the payer policy adopts it.
