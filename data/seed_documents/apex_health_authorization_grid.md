# Apex Health Plan — Authorization & Referral Grid (Synthetic)

**Document:** AHP-AUTH-2023-07  
**Scope:** Out-of-network referrals and prior authorization for specialty services.

## Key rules
- Services listed on the AHP “PA required” grid must have an approved authorization number on the claim.
- Retro-authorization is only permitted within 72 hours for emergent encounters with attestation.

## Common denial reasons
- Missing authorization number
- Authorization expired prior to date of service
- Mismatch between authorized CPT and billed CPT

## Appeal strategy (internal)
Include authorization screenshots, call reference IDs, and corrected CPT mapping tables when applicable.
