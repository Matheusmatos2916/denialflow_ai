## Purpose and limitations

This document defines a documentation checklist for a synthetic imaging policy. It does not establish coverage, medical necessity, or an authorization requirement for any real payer. The policy version and benefit in force on the date of service control.

Prior authorization, medical necessity, and payment are separate decisions. An authorization, when required and obtained, does not by itself guarantee payment.

## Required clinical record

For elective outpatient advanced imaging, the record should identify:

- presenting symptoms, duration, severity, and relevant examination findings;
- working diagnosis and clinical question the imaging is intended to answer;
- prior treatment, observation, or conservative management when clinically appropriate;
- response, failure, contraindication, or intolerance to prior treatment;
- relevant laboratory, radiology, or specialist findings;
- requested modality, body region, contrast use, and site of service;
- ordering clinician and date of the order;
- applicable authorization or referral reference, when required by the product.

“Failed conservative management” is not a universal requirement. The applicable clinical policy must define the required duration, exceptions, and alternatives for the requested service.

## Pre-service verification

Before scheduling or billing, verify eligibility, covered benefit, network status, authorization requirement, approved modality and body region, place of service, and authorization validity dates. Record the policy title, version, effective date, and source used for the decision.

## Denial classification

| Denial type | Evidence to review | Possible response |
|---|---|---|
| Medical necessity | History, examination, prior treatment, clinical question | Map each policy criterion to a record document |
| Missing authorization | Approval record, portal transaction, benefit rules | Confirm whether authorization was required for this product and date |
| Code or site mismatch | Ordered, authorized, and billed code/site | Correct claim or request reconsideration |
| Incomplete records | Missing note, result, order, or report | Submit the missing dated record |

## Appeal evidence checklist

Submit the denial, relevant history and examination, treatment history, imaging order, clinical indication, applicable test results, authorization record when relevant, and the exact policy or guideline section supporting the request. Avoid sending unrelated records or citing a guideline without its title, version, and applicability.
