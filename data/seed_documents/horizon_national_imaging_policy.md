# Horizon National — Clinical Policy Memo (Synthetic)

**Policy ID:** HN-CLM-2024-118  
**Topic:** Medical necessity documentation for advanced imaging (CT/MRI) in outpatient setting.

## Summary
Horizon National may deny advanced imaging when documentation does not clearly establish medical necessity relative to conservative care and clinical presentation.

## Requirements
- Progress notes must reference failed conservative management where clinically appropriate.
- Authorization must be on file for elective advanced imaging when member benefit requires it.

## Appeal guidance
Appeals should include: relevant history, prior treatments, clinical indication for imaging, and any applicable guidelines cited by the plan.
